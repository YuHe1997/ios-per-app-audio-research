# Phase 3 — Signal Path

## Goal

Characterize volume dependence, feedback/self-capture and digital timing.

## Observed data

Captured digital PCM was broadly insensitive to physical output-volume position in the valid comparisons. Audio rendered by the capture App could re-enter the capture stream; the attempted current-process exclusion did not remove it in the tested route. Render/capture digital timing was stable enough for offline analysis, and known self-playback references could be strongly subtracted offline.

## Result

The route behaves like a digital system mix rather than microphone pickup, but self-feedback must be handled by research clients.

## Does not prove

Reference subtraction for audio generated by this App does not separate arbitrary third-party music and game sources.

## Reproduce

Use synthetic tones/PRBS, record operation timestamps, verify actual output volume, and compare spectra rather than listening alone.
